This build is assembled entirely from open, redistributable sources: species-level divergence times from Smith & Brown 2018 (seed plants), Nitta et al. 2022 (ferns), Rabosky et al. 2018 (ray-finned fishes), Upham et al. 2019 (mammals) and Varga et al. 2019 (mushrooms), hung on a backbone of deep-node ages from Strassert et al. 2021 and vertebrate nodes from Irisarri et al. 2017. Species without a source tree are placed next to a congener or family member, or by a hand-curated anchor; a few backbone nodes are still marked approximate. Birds, reptiles, amphibians, sharks, molluscs, crustaceans, insects and seaweeds are the groups still being added.
