This build is assembled entirely from open, redistributable sources: species-level divergence times from Smith & Brown 2018 (seed plants), Nitta et al. 2022 (ferns), Rabosky et al. 2018 (ray-finned fishes), Upham et al. 2019 (mammals), Jetz et al. 2012 (birds), Stein et al. 2018 (sharks and rays), Jetz & Pyron 2018 (amphibians), Tonini et al. 2016 (squamates) and Varga et al. 2019 (mushrooms), hung on a backbone of deep-node ages from Strassert et al. 2021 and vertebrate nodes from Irisarri et al. 2017. Molluscs, crustaceans, insects, seaweeds, ascomycete fungi, turtles, crocodilians and the small phyla have no species-level open chronogram yet; their families sit on a class- and order-level skeleton whose ages are cited where a dated phylogenomic study exists (decapods: Wolfe et al. 2019) and otherwise approximate and flagged for review (see node_review_open.csv). Placements on that skeleton join at the order's crown, which slightly overstates PD for those groups.
